## Wed 14 Oct — `V5`: reversal-detector rules

- **Satisfies:** `FR-EM-V5` rows 1–4. **Files:** `reversal.py`, extend `tests/test_reversal_patterns.py`.
- **The four rules:** H&S top (heavy left shoulder, decidedly lighter right, **head volume

  neckline penetration **required**, the strongest penalty in the set, and explicitly **not**
