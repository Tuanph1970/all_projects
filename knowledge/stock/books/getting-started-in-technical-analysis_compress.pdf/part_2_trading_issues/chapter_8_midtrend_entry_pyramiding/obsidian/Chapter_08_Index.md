**Part:** Two — Trading Issues
**Date read:** 2026-05-01

---

## Why this chapter exists

Most chart books cover trend identification but skip the practical question every trader actually faces:
**"The market has already moved a lot — is it too late to get in?"**

Schwager's answer: usually no. Refusing to enter a clear trend just because it's already moved is one of the most common mistakes. The chapter gives four concrete techniques for entering *during* a trend, then shows that the same techniques double as triggers for **pyramiding** (adding to a winning position).

## Key takeaway in one sentence

> Pauses and corrections inside a trend are the safest entry points; the four techniques in this chapter are just different definitions of "the pause is over, the trend is back."
