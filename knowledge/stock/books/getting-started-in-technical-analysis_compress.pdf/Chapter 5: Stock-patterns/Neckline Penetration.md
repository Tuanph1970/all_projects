# Neckline Penetration (H&S Completion)

## Definition
The neckline connects the reaction lows (H&S top) or reaction highs (H&S bottom). The H&S is NOT complete until the neckline is penetrated.

## Why the Neckline Matters
Without it, the H&S is just three bumps. Many uptrends produce a pattern that *looks* like H&S but never breaks the neckline — the trend simply continues. The neckline break is the structural confirmation that the trend is actually over.

## Neckline Geometry
- **Horizontal** — both reaction lows at same level (cleanest)
- **Sloping up** — second reaction low higher (slightly less bearish — still making higher lows)
- **Sloping down** — second reaction low lower (more bearish — already making lower lows)

## For Algorithmic Detection
Use the **higher** of the two reaction lows as a conservative neckline (requires deeper penetration). The sloping neckline case can be modeled as a line fit, but Schwager doesn't prescribe this — he uses the simpler horizontal approximation.

## Same Trade-off as Double Top Completion
Deep necklines → late confirmation → use clue-stacking at the right shoulder instead.
