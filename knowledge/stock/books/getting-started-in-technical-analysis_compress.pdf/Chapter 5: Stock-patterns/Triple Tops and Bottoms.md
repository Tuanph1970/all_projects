# Triple Tops and Bottoms

## Definition
Three peaks (or troughs) at approximately the same level. An extension of the double top/bottom.

## Analogy
Three shots at the same basketball hoop from the same spot, all hitting the rim. Three failed attempts at the same price level — the market exhausts itself and reverses.

## Schwager's Treatment
Brief: "occur rather infrequently, but would be interpreted in the same fashion" as double tops/bottoms. All the same rules apply — confirmation via reaction low/high breach, clue-stacking for anticipation, and failed-signal logic from Chapter 11.

## Why Three Tests > Two Tests
Each failed attempt at the same level strengthens the evidence that the level is real resistance/support. The third failure is more damning than the second because the market had an additional opportunity to break through and still couldn't.
