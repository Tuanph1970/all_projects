# Chapter 6 — Oscillators (Index)

> Source: Schwager, Jack D. — *Getting Started in Technical Analysis*, Chapter 6 (pp. 110–119)

## What this chapter is about

Oscillators are price-based mathematical indicators used mainly as **countertrend** tools — they aim to spot short-term reversal points rather than long-term trends. Almost every popular oscillator (Momentum, ROC, MACD, RSI, Stochastics) is just a different way to measure one underlying idea: **momentum** (the rate at which prices change).

## Key chapter insight

Oscillator-based countertrend signals work **wonderfully in trading ranges and disastrously in trending markets**. Schwager's recommendation: treat oscillator signals as *alerts* — wait for **price** itself to confirm a reversal before acting.
