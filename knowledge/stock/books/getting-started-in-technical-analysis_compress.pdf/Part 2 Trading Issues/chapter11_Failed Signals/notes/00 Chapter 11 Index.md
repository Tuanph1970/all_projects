# Chapter 11 — The Most Important Rule in Chart Analysis

> *"The market is like a flu virus — as soon as you think you have it pegged, it mutates into something else."* — Wayne H. Wagner (chapter epigraph)

**Source:** Schwager, *Getting Started in Technical Analysis*, Chapter 11 (pp. 152–177)
**Master concept:** [[01 Failed Signals]]
**Big idea:** when a market refuses to follow through on what a chart pattern predicts, that *refusal itself* is one of the most reliable signals you can trade — often more reliable than the original pattern.

## The Schwager hierarchy of trader skill (his closing remark)

1. **Novice trader** — ignores a failed signal; rides the loss hoping for the best.
2. **Experienced trader** — applies money management; exits quickly once the trade is wrong.
3. **Skilled trader** — does a 180° turn and *reverses* the position when behaviour proves the signal failed.

Failed-signal trading sits at level 3.
